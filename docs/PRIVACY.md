# Guldr privacy policy

**Effective:** October 23, 2026

Guldr is a personal finance app for iPhone that keeps everything on your device. There is no account to create, Guldr has no servers, and nothing you enter is sent to us or to anyone else.

## What Guldr stores

Everything you enter stays in Guldr's storage on your iPhone:
- transactions, categories and budgets;
- the days you logged, your streak and your achievements;
- your preferences, such as your currency, the lock, reminders and widget privacy.

Guldr's widgets read the same data on your iPhone to show your summary. Like any app's data, it is included in your iPhone's backups (iCloud Backup or a computer) if you have them turned on, and it is restored with them.

## What Guldr doesn't do

- **No collection.** We don't receive your data. The app has no networking.
- **No analytics or crash reporting** of our own, and no third-party SDKs.
- **No advertising and no tracking** across apps or websites.
- **No account,** no sign-in, no email address.

If you choose to share crash reports and analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple shares crash data with us under its own terms. It never includes what you entered in Guldr.

## Face ID

The optional lock uses Face ID or your passcode through iOS. Guldr only learns whether you were recognized; your face data never leaves the Secure Enclave and Guldr can't read it.

## Notifications

Reminders and budget alerts are scheduled by Guldr on your iPhone; no server sends them. The daily reminder never shows amounts, and budget alerts leave amounts out while the Face ID lock is on, since notifications can appear on your lock screen.

## When you share something

Data leaves Guldr only when you choose to send it:
- **CSV export:** a file with the transactions you pick, sent wherever you choose in the share sheet.
- **Share cards:** an image of an achievement or of a month, sent wherever you choose. It never shows amounts.
- **Contact support:** an email you write and send, which includes the app and iOS versions.

What happens after that is up to the app or person you send it to.

## Deleting your data

**Settings › Delete all data** erases everything in Guldr at once. Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone; copies in older backups remain until those backups are replaced.

## Children

Guldr isn't directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date. A change that sends data off your device would be explained in the app first, and would never apply to data you entered before it.
