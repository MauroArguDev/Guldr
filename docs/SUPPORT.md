# Guldr support

Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Write to [argudev@icloud.com](mailto:argudev@icloud.com). **Settings › About › Contact support** in the app opens an email that already includes the app and iOS versions. Replies usually take one or two business days.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is my data?
Only on your iPhone. Guldr has no account and no servers; see the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).

### Does Guldr sync between devices?
Not yet. iCloud sync is planned for a future version. Until then, your data moves to a new iPhone with an iCloud or computer backup, like other app data.

### How do I back up my transactions?
Guldr's data is part of your iPhone's backups. For a copy you can open anywhere, use **Settings › Export CSV**: it creates a spreadsheet file with the transactions you choose.

### Can I use more than one currency?
Every transaction keeps the currency it was saved in. Totals, budgets and charts use the active currency, which you can change in **Settings › Currency**; transactions in other currencies aren't added into those totals.

### How does the streak work?
A day counts when you log a transaction dated that day, or mark it as **No spending**, by the end of the next day, so a forgotten day can still be repaired the morning after. Every 7 days in a row earn a freeze, up to 2, which covers a missed day automatically. Tap the streak on Home to see your calendar.

### Why don't I see a medal I expected?
Money achievements, such as budgets and savings, only count complete months: months that are over and have at least 90 % of their days logged. That way a medal never rewards leaving expenses out. Medals you earn are kept, even if you later delete transactions.

### The daily reminder doesn't arrive.
Check that **Daily reminder** is on in Guldr's **Settings › Notifications**, and that notifications for Guldr are allowed in the iPhone's Settings app. The reminder is skipped on days you've already logged.

### How do I hide my amounts?
- **Face ID lock:** **Settings › Security** locks the app when it opens or comes back, and keeps amounts out of budget alerts.
- **Widgets:** **Hide amounts in widgets** in the same section hides amounts on the Home Screen, with or without the lock.

### How do I change the language?
Guldr follows your iPhone's language (English or Spanish). To use a different one just for Guldr, open the iPhone's **Settings › Apps › Guldr › Language**.

### How do I delete everything?
**Settings › Delete all data** erases all your transactions, budgets, streak, achievements and preferences. It can't be undone, so export a CSV first if you want a copy.
